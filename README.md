🌒 Light Shadows

Contributers : Shafiei Armin - Scrob Sebastian - Turcu Mihai Alexandru - Hajian Danial (1231EA - Sgr 2)

For centuries, the Kingdom of Eloria lived in peace under the protection of the Order of the Moon Knights, an ancient brotherhood sworn to defend the realm from the dark magic warlocks. These evil entities, known for corrupting nature and bending spirits to their will, had been sealed away long ago. But darkness is patient. One night, the Great Warlock Morgrath broke free from his eternal prison. With his return, the land began to wither, storms twisted unnaturally in the sky, and terrifying creatures rose from the soil. One by one, the Moon Knights fell; ambushed, hunted and  erased. All except one. Alden, the youngest initiate of the Order, inexperienced and untested, survived only because he was sent away to train in a secluded village. Now, with the Order in ruins, he is the last remaining Moon Knight of our story… whether he is ready or not. Before his mentor died, Alden was given the Lunar Seal, a sacred artifact that can reveal the Warlocks’ hidden path. But the Seal will only respond to a mind that is focused and unwavering. Alden must prove he is worthy of carrying the Seal and continuing the legacy of his fallen Order.

🌟 Task 1 – The Memory Seal

Alden stands in the dim light of the old training sanctuary. In front of his eyes, a magic forms a shifting pattern (a code of symbols used by the Moon Knights to test clarity of mind.)

His mentor’s final words echo:
“A Moon Knight must first command his thoughts before he commands a blade.
Remember the sequence. Repeat it. Only then will the Seal accept you as its bearer.”

The Seal flashes a mystical code.
Alden must memorize it, then enter it correctly.
If he succeeds, the Seal will guid him to the next level, where Alden’s next challenge awaits.

🔗 Task 2 – Sword Training

With the Seal now recognizing Alden, its soft pulse guides him forward.
But knowledge alone is not enough.
To survive the Warlocks’ creations, Alden must sharpen his combat skills.
Through the opened gate lies the arena where generations of knights trained their swordsmanship. Seal take him to a weak evil wizard, Alden tightens his grip on his training blade.
The real journey is about to begin.
