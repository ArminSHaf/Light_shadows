🌒 Prologue – Shadows Over Eloria

For centuries, the Kingdom of Eloria lived in peace under the protection of the Order of the Moon Knights, an ancient brotherhood sworn to defend the realm from the dark magic of the Warlocks. These sorcerers, known for corrupting nature and bending spirits to their will, had been sealed away long ago.

But darkness is patient.

One fateful night, the Great Warlock Morgrath broke free from his eternal prison. With his return, the land began to wither, storms twisted unnaturally in the sky, and terrifying creatures rose from the soil.
One by one, the Moon Knights fell — ambushed, hunted, erased.

All except one.

Alden, the youngest initiate of the Order, inexperienced and untested, survived only because he was sent away to train in a secluded village. Now, with the Order in ruins, he is the last remaining Moon Knight… whether he is ready or not.

Before he died, Alden’s mentor entrusted him with the Lunar Seal, a sacred artifact that can reveal the Warlocks’ hidden path. But the Seal will only respond to a mind that is focused and unwavering.

Alden must prove he is worthy of carrying the Seal and continuing the legacy of his fallen Order.

⸻

🌟 Task 1 – The Memory Seal

Alden stands in the dim light of the old training sanctuary, the air heavy with incense and the lingering presence of his mentor.
Before him floats the Lunar Seal, glowing with pale silver light. Its magic forms a shifting pattern — a code of symbols used by the Moon Knights to test clarity of mind.

His mentor’s final words echo:

“A Moon Knight must first command his thoughts before he commands a blade.
Remember the sequence. Repeat it. Only then will the Seal accept you as its bearer.”

The Seal flashes a mystical code.
Alden must memorize it, then enter it correctly.
If he succeeds, the Seal stabilizes, binding itself to him — a sign that he is now officially on the path to becoming a true Moon Knight.

As the Seal locks into place, a stone gate ahead rumbles open, revealing the Training Grounds, where Alden’s next challenge awaits.

⸻

🔗 Transition Toward Task 2 – Sword Training

With the Seal now recognizing Alden, its soft pulse guides him forward.
But knowledge alone is not enough.
To survive the Warlocks’ creations, Alden must sharpen his combat skills.

Through the opened gate lies the arena where generations of knights trained their swordsmanship. Wooden dummies rise from the ground, moving targets awaken, and ancient mechanisms begin to hum.

Alden tightens his grip on his training blade.

The real journey is about to begin.
